## Risk Notice

**Read carefully. You may lose part or all of the value you use.**

- **Volatility:** crypto prices can fall sharply in a short time.

- **Fake tokens and scams:** anyone can create a token with the same name as a well-known project. Always check the contract address.

- **Impermanent loss:** liquidity providers may end up with less value than if they had simply held the tokens.

- **Price range:** outside the chosen range, a position stops earning and is 100% in one token.

- **Smart contracts:** even audited code can have flaws exploited by others.

- **Irreversible transactions:** sending to a wrong address or approving a malicious contract cannot be undone.

- **Wallet keys:** losing your recovery phrase means losing your funds; revealing it can get them stolen.

- **Price and bots:** the final price may move up to your tolerance limit; bots may front-run trades.

- **Networks and regulation:** networks can become unstable and crypto rules can change.
