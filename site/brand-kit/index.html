About KAMBIODEX

KAMBIODEX is a decentralized exchange (DEX) on Polygon. People swap tokens straight from their own wallet and can provide liquidity to pools. The name is always written KAMBIODEX: one word, all capitals, never translated.

The logo is the symbol plus the KAMBIODEX wordmark, with “DEX” in gold and a rising gold line below. Use the version that fits the background.

Usage rules

Do
  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the width of the symbol.
  • Minimum size: logo 100 px wide (25 mm in print); symbol 24 px (8 mm).
  • Use the dark version on dark backgrounds.
Don't
  • Do not stretch, rotate or recolor the logo.
  • Do not add shadows or effects.
  • Do not place the logo on busy or colored backgrounds.
  • Do not crop the symbol into a circle.
  • Do not write the name as “KambioDex”, “Kambio DEX” or “Kambio-Dex”.
