KAMBIODEX

How to use the KAMBIODEX logo, symbol, colors, typefaces and icons on the website, on social media and on listing sites.

for internal and partner use

01 · logo

The logo

The logo combines the symbol (two circling arrows with a gold dot at the center) and the KAMBIODEX wordmark, with “DEX” in gold. Always write the name as one word, in capitals.

primary · light backgrounds

dark backgrounds

one color · black

one color · white

When to use each version

  • Primary: whenever possible, on light backgrounds.
  • Dark background: in the site’s dark theme, on banners and dark photos.
  • One color: when color is not available, such as stamps, engravings and black-and-white documents.

02 · symbol

The symbol

The two arrows stand for continuous swapping between tokens; the gold dot at the center is the pool, where the swap happens. The symbol can be used alone when space is tight: browser icon, app icon, profile picture and listings.

primary

dark background

no square

Clear space

Keep a clear area of at least half the width of the symbol’s square around the logo and the symbol. No text, image or border may enter this area.

Square corners

The symbol’s square has sharp corners, matching the site’s banking look. Platforms that round icons automatically (iPhone, Android, social networks) may do so; in our own materials, keep the corners square.

05 · misuse

What not to do

do not stretch or squash
do not change the colors
do not rotate
do not add shadows or effects
do not place on busy or colored backgrounds
do not round the square in our own materials

Brand name

  • Always write KAMBIODEX: one word, all capitals. Not “KambioDex”, “Kambio DEX” or “Kambio-Dex”.
  • The name is never translated: it is KAMBIODEX in every language.

Interface icons

For icons inside the site (gear, arrows, moon and sun, wallet), use a single thin-line, open-license library such as Lucide, with stroke width 2 and rounded line caps, matching the symbol.
